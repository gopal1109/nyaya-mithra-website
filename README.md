# Nyaya Mithra (nyaya-mithra-website)

**Nyaya Mithra** is an AI-powered legal awareness and grievance support platform designed to assist citizens in understanding their legal rights, identifying unfair commercial practices, disputing restaurant service charges or traffic challans, and connecting with official government complaint channels.

---

## 🌟 Key Features

* **Legal Rights Awareness:** Instant insights into Indian consumer rights and legal protections.
* **Grievance Resolution Assistant:** Step-by-step guidance on disputing incorrect traffic challans, overcharges, and service fees.
* **Official Channel Navigation:** Direct access links and procedures for official complaint platforms (e.g., National Consumer Helpline, NCH).
* **AI-Powered Queries:** Easy-to-use interface powered by AI for quick answers to complex legal questions.

---

## 🛠️ Tech Stack

* **Frontend:** HTML, CSS, JavaScript / React
* **Backend:** Node.js / Express or Python
* **AI Integration:** Google Gemini API / Google AI Studio
